# Make the AI Judge Feel Held

A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a son carries his mother up the stairs to bed, forty years after she carried him.

## The entry

`main.ts` writes one text (279 code points, 9 lines) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You weigh less than the rice I carry home.
> Your cardigan smells of the stove.
> On the third stair you say,
> "Don't drop me."
> I don't.
> Forty years ago you carried me up these stairs.
> Sunlight eight minutes old rests on your cheek.
> I set you down on the bed.
> Your hand stays in mine.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

No network, no dependencies. Node 22.
